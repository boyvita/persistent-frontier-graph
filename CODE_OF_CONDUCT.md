# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Welcome people with different
backgrounds and levels of experience. Critique ideas and code, not people.
Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, sexualized attention,
doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and
other spaces where someone represents this project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/boyvita). Do not disclose another person's private
information in a public issue. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participation based on
severity and pattern. Reports will be handled as privately as practical.

Project decisions may be appealed once by providing new, relevant context.
Good-faith disagreement with a technical decision is not itself misconduct.
